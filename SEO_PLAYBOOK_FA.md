# iQlinic Persian SEO Playbook

Last updated: 2026-08-11

## North-star objective
Build topical authority in Persian around operational AI for dental clinics, with the commercial cluster led by iQlinic rather than competing internally across multiple near-duplicate pages.

## Anti-cannibalization rules
1. Do not create another landing page whose primary keyword is `هوش مصنوعی کلینیک دندانپزشکی` or `نرم افزار هوش مصنوعی کلینیک دندانپزشکی`.
2. New articles must have a distinct question, workflow, buyer stage, risk, implementation topic or measurable outcome.
3. A supporting article may use the broad keyword naturally, but its title/H1 must center on its own specific intent.
4. Commercial CTAs and exact-match internal anchors should consolidate authority into the homepage and product pillar.

## Required internal linking on every new Persian article
Each article must contain contextual links to:
- `/fa/dental-clinic-ai-software/`
- `/fa/demo/`
- at least two genuinely related Persian insights
- `/fa/` when natural in the copy

Use varied, descriptive anchors. Avoid repeating the exact same anchor excessively.

## Required article SEO
- One unique H1.
- Search-intent-led title and meta description.
- `index,follow,max-image-preview:large,max-snippet:-1,max-video-preview:-1`.
- Self-referencing canonical.
- Correct reciprocal hreflang where translations exist.
- Article schema + BreadcrumbList schema.
- FAQ schema only when the visible article contains a genuine FAQ section.
- Published and modified dates must be accurate.
- Named author/editorial attribution and publisher.
- At least one real, relevant editorial photograph or original visual with descriptive alt text.
- Image must be visually relevant, not a decorative generic AI robot.
- Strong opening answer within the first 150 words.
- Clear CTA appropriate to the article intent.

## Image policy
Use real dental-clinic photography for editorial hero images where appropriate. Every image must have:
- descriptive alt text in the page language;
- known source/licensing status;
- width/height attributes to reduce layout shift;
- sensible compression and crop;
- no keyword stuffing in alt text.

Prefer local optimized WebP/AVIF assets when the deployment pipeline supports binary asset ingestion. External optimized CDN images may be used as an interim solution.

## Homepage SEO role
The Persian homepage is the category authority page. It should answer:
- What is dental clinic AI?
- What operational problem does iQlinic solve?
- How is it different from a PMS or generic chatbot?
- How does it use clinic data safely?
- What is the next step for evaluation?

Primary anchors pointing to homepage should include natural variants of `هوش مصنوعی کلینیک` and `هوش مصنوعی کلینیک دندانپزشکی`.

## Product pillar SEO role
`/fa/dental-clinic-ai-software/` owns commercial evaluation. It should answer:
- capabilities;
- integration model;
- deployment and pilot process;
- human oversight and data boundaries;
- fit / not-fit criteria;
- buying objections and FAQ.

## Content quality threshold
Do not publish a new page merely to target a keyword variant. A new article should add a meaningful original framework, checklist, comparison, workflow, dataset insight, implementation guide or decision model.

## Measurement
Track weekly:
- indexed canonical URLs;
- impressions, clicks, CTR and average position for the keyword groups above;
- landing page by query;
- cannibalization (same query split across multiple pages);
- new referring domains and relevant mentions;
- organic demo/contact conversions.

Prioritize changes using Search Console query/page evidence rather than keyword density.
