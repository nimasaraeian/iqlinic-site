THE REAL CLINIC PROBLEM

Patients do not always disappear with a single cancellation.

Drop-off often happens between systems: an open plan, no next appointment, an unresolved payment question, or an unusual gap that no one notices in time.

01

Context is fragmented

Operationally important information lives across appointments, treatment, payments and communications.

02

Follow-up is reactive

Teams often notice the problem after the patient has already been inactive for too long.

03

Priority is unclear

A long call list does not tell the team who deserves attention today, or why.

A DECISION LAYER, NOT ANOTHER PMS

Move from “what happened?” to “what should we review now?”

iQlinic is designed to sit on top of existing clinic data, build patient context, surface explainable recovery signals and help teams prioritize action.

Recovery Center

  • Prioritizes patients who need reviewUsing multiple signals instead of a single recency rule
  • Shows why each patient was flaggedSo the team can challenge or accept the signal
  • Suggests the next actionCall, message, resolve a blocker, schedule or escalate for human review

Patient 360

  • Connects care journey contextVisits, appointments, treatment, payment and follow-up history
  • Makes unusual gaps visibleFocus on continuity, not retrospective reporting alone
  • Supports real decisionsLess information, but more relevant information at the decision point

EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIMS

No performance theater.

The public demo is synthetic. Real impact metrics only make sense after connecting to a clinic's data and establishing a baseline.

What a pilot should measure

Recovery opportunityHow many flagged patients are actually actionable?
Action completionHow often does the team complete prioritized actions?
Return / continuation outcomeWhat changes after the action?
False-positive reviewWhich signals were not useful, and why?
Does iQlinic replace clinic management software?

No. The focus is the intelligence and decision layer. The existing operational system remains in place.

Does the public demo use real patient data?

No. Every patient, event and number in the public demo is synthetic.

Can every clinic connect immediately?

No. Integration depends on the source system, permissions and data quality. A technical feasibility check comes first.
